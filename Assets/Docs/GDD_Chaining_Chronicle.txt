1. Game Overview
1.1 Game Concept
Provide a high-level summary of the game idea, its core mechanics, and unique selling points. Describe the inspiration or vision behind the game and what experience it aims to deliver to players.
The game Chaining Chronicle aspires to be a roguelike with permadeath mechanics. You start out on relatively easy stages that proceed to get harder as you continue on. The main selling point would be the twist on the combat system. Unlike within a traditional turn-based combat system attacks can chain into another and even other party members can join the chain if the afflicted status matches their follow up condition.

1.2 Genre
Define the genre(s) of the game (e.g., action, role-playing, puzzle), explaining how it aligns with the overall concept.

The Chaining Chronicle is a rogue like with turn based combat mechanics. Its exploration could be described as a dungeon crawler similar to the mystery dungeon series.
1.3 Target Audience
Identify the primary audience based on demographics, interests, and gaming habits. Specify age group, skill level, and any specific preferences or needs.

The primary audience would be fans of turn based RPGs that search for a fresh twist during combat and also like rouge likes
1.4 Game Flow Summary
Describe the progression of the game from start to finish. Include how the player transitions between different stages, modes, or levels. 

The player starts on a randomly generated stage with stairs to the next stage and enemies scouting the area. When the tile of the player overlaps with the tile an enemy is on, combat starts. Combat is done when the enemy or the player is defeated. Then the progression through the stages continues. On these stages upgrades like armor and chaining skills can be found


1.5 Look and Feel
Summarize the intended aesthetic style, including graphical tone, art direction, and mood. Mention inspirations or themes (e.g., futuristic, fantasy).

Chaining Chronicle will be a pixel-art based game inspired by the likes of Zelda Links Awakening, and early Dragon Quest. Concretely this comprises of a top down 2d view of the stages to explore and a 2d combat screen
2. Gameplay and Mechanics
2.1 Gameplay
2.1.1 Game Progression
Outline how players advance in the game, including level structure, storyline progression, or open-world exploration.

Players fight through stages and try to achieve the highest Stage number in s high score approach. The MVP will not feature a storyline.
2.1.2 Mission / challenge Structure
Detail how missions or challenges are organized, including objectives, conditions for success, and replayability.

The MVP will only feature the “Stage Attack” mode which represents the main gameplay of fighting through stages to reach a new highscore
2.1.3 Puzzle Structure
If applicable, explain how puzzles are designed, their types, and how they integrate into the gameplay.

The MVP will not feature puzzles
2.1.4 Objectives
List the primary and secondary objectives for players, such as completing quests, solving puzzles, or defeating enemies.

The main objective is to reach a highscore stage. To achieve this the player will have to collect powerups, to defeat enemies standing in the way.
2.1.5 Play Flow
Describe the expected rhythm and pacing of the gameplay, including combat intensity, exploration phases, or moments of rest.

While Chaining Chronicle has a small exploration aspect via trying to find the stage exit and power ups while doing so, the main focus will definitely be combat. While trying to progress you will have to fight and try to stay alive a lot. The player also has to wager if fighting for the experience points is worth it or too risky regarding their health pool.
2.2 Mechanics
2.2.1 . Physics 
Explain how the game incorporates physical interactions, such as gravity, collisions, or fluid dynamics.

Chaining chronicle will only feature simple physics like collision with walls and enemies. There will be no other representation of physics
2.2.2 Movement in the game 
Detail how players or objects move, including controls, animations, and any unique traversal mechanics.

The movement will be grid-based similar to the (Pokémon-) Mystery Dungeon series. In a later version there could also be power ups that let the Player traverse different terrain like water, or chasms
2.2.3 Objects 
Describe how you interact with objects in the game.

Enemies encountered on the current stage are interacted with via collision by standing on the same tile. Other Objects like power ups are interacted with by standing on a adjacent tile, facing the object and hitting the interact key
2.2.4 Actions 
Summarize the range of player actions, such as running, jumping, interacting, or special abilities.

The Player can only move and interact with the environment via the “interact key”. The interact key is context sensitive and can lead to different outcomes regarding the circumstance it is used in.


2.2.5 Combat 
If applicable, outline the combat system, including weapons, skills, or tactics used by the player.

When entering combat, usually the player gets to start. If a player has multiple members in their party the member with the highest speed stat starts.

2.2.6 Economy 
Describe in-game resources, currencies, or trading systems and how they impact gameplay.

In the MVP version of the game the only resources are the power ups that can be found and the skill slots they are allotted to. Currently there are no plans of implementing currency, shops or trading. Though in the future there might be a skill tree for permanent progression.
2.2.7 Screen Flow 
Explain transitions between menus, gameplay screens, and other UI elements.
Mockups
The main screen transitions are from the stage to the combat screen and back. Here the HUD will be altered greatly. Another transition could be from the stage to a menu but this will not be a focus of the MVP
2.3 . Game Options 
List available customization options, such as difficulty settings, visual tweaks, or control mappings.

Options are not a priority in the MVP. There will be one fixed difficulty setting. Controls will be rather simple and not a focus point, but mappings should be offered. 
2.4  Replaying and Saving 
Detail how the game supports save files, checkpoints, and replayability (e.g., multiple endings or new game modes).

As Chaining Chronicle does not offer any meta progression in the MVP the only thing that will be stored permanently is the leaderboard.
2.5 Cheats and Easter Eggs 
Optionally, describe hidden content, cheats, or playful secrets for players to discover.

Not initially planned but might be added later on. Maybe a dev mode could be implemented to test features and balancing
3. Story, Setting and Character 
3.1 Story and Narrative 
Summarize the game's story, narrative structure, and key plot points. Highlight themes and emotional impact.

Chaining Chronicle will not feature story or exposition in the MVP.
3.2 Game World 
3.2.1 General look and feel of world 
Describe the overall world design, including aesthetic style, atmosphere, and tone.

As there is no cohesive world but rather many stages, each stage can represent another environment. This will be achieved through pallet swapping the pixel art tiles. A small hub world to check the leaderboards is the only place except for the stages.
3.2.2 Areas 
Provide an overview of different areas or locations in the game, their purpose, and distinct characteristics.

See 3.2.1
3.3 Characters 
List major characters, including protagonists, antagonists, and NPCs. Describe their roles, backstories, and personalities

There will be no character exposition in the MVP. The player should be able to name his character for the run and other found party members will have their name allocated by a predefined pool.



4. Levels 

4.1 Levels. 
Describe the level structure, design principles, and layout. Mention how levels tie into the gameplay and story.

Chaining Chronicle will offer a endless amount of randomly generated stages that increase in difficulty.
4.2 Training Level / Tutorial
Explain the purpose and content of the tutorial or training level. Highlight key mechanics introduced to players.

Chaining Chronicle does not need a Tutorial level as it strives to be a learning by doing experience like many other roguelikes.



5. Interface 

5.1 Visual System
Detail the visual layout of menus, HUD (Heads-Up Display), and other interface elements.

All in all the HUD will look pretty similar to games like siralim Ultimate and only feature the strict necessities. While exploring not much is needed only notification if something extraordinary is happening on this stage.
In Combat you will get an overview of your health as well as the enemies health and Menues to pick your actions. Also a combat log which documents the happening of the previous turns could be implemented.

Explain the control scheme, including input devices and key bindings.

Controls will be rather simple as the game is set in a 2D world and focuses on combat and not interesting movements. The Control scheme will look something like:

5.3 Audio, music, sound effects 
Summarize the role of audio in creating the game's atmosphere. Mention specific design goals for music and sound effects.

The MVP will not focus on music or sound effects. Only the most common actions will have corresponding sound effects as a proof of concept. Per Stage a different track of copyright free music could be played (no separate combat theme in the MVP).
5.4 Help System 
Describe tools available to assist players, such as tutorials, hints, or documentation.

The Chaining Chronicle itself could act as an tutorial book that explains mechanics and interactions within the game.
6. Artificial Intelligence 
6.1 Opponent and Enemy AI
Describe how enemy characters behave, including attack strategies and adaptive tactics.

Enemies basically work pretty much exactly like the player-controlled characters but instead of the player choosing a move something like a finite state machine will control the enemy in the MVP. This will be very basic, something like if player character is in kill range target that player character or if own health <30% prioritize healing moves. If this proves to be too complex moves will be chosen randomly. 

Enemies will also need an AI to move through the stages and seek the player, and not run into walls etc.
6.2 Non-combat and Friendly Characters 
Explain the behaviors of NPCs, companions, or other non-hostile entities.

Currently no friendly NPCs are planned.
6.3 Support AI 
Detail any AI systems that assist the player, such as auto-aim or hint systems.
Currently not planned.
7. Technical 
7.1 Target Hardware 
List the platforms the game will support, such as PC, consoles, or mobile devices.
The target hardware will only be PCs. Chaining Chronicle aims to be runnable on every machine and should not require hefty amounts of computing power



7.2 Development hardware and software, including Game Engine 
Describe the tools, technologies, and game engines used for development.

The game will be built in Unity Engine. Aseprite will be used for pixel art and all other graphical necessities
7.3 Network requirements 
Mention online capabilities, such as multiplayer, cloud saving, or leaderboards.
An online leaderboard will be aspired

9. Requirements
9.1 Must
List non-negotiable requirements essential to the game's functionality.
Chaining Chronicle must provide the player with continuous stages that can be explored
Chaining Chronicle must provide the player with the ability to combat enemies
Chaining Chronicle must provide the player with the ability to collect power ups
Chaining Chronicle must provide an persistent offline leaderboard to keep track of high scores.
When exploring the stage, Chaining Chronicle must implement player movement and collision
When exploring the stage, Chaining Chronicle must implement enemy AI-movement and collision

9.2 Should
Mention important but flexible requirements.

Chaining Chronicle should provide basic sound effect handling

Chaining Chronicle should provide basic music handling

Chaining Chronicle should be runnable on every modern PC and should not require a dedicated graphics card or high end System


9.3 Could
Describe optional features or enhancements.
Chaining Chronicle could provide dedicated music and sound effects

For general settings, or quitting the game, Chaining Chronicle could provide a menu, which can be accessed while on a stage.
